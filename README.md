SFF Graphics Card Guide: 4 Best GPUs for Mini-ITX Cases
Small cases get hot. A graphics card that looks fine on a spec sheet can struggle in a mini-ITX build, where space is tight and air has fewer places to go. This guide compares four SFF graphics cards on length, power draw, and cooler design, to help you pick one that fits your case. On paper, the ASUS Dual RTX 4070 offers the best balance of size and cooling. I did not test these cards myself, and the specs come from the manufacturers.

How this guide was made
This is a buying guide, not a hands-on test. I compared four cards using manufacturer specs: length, TDP (power draw), and cooler type. Specs and prices can change, so check the product page before you buy. To see how I label my articles, read How We Test/Review .

Why length, power, and cooling matter in a small case
In a small case, less air moves around the card, so heat builds up faster. Many graphics cards slow down when they get too hot. This is called thermal throttling. Most overlays show the core temperature, but cards also have other sensors, such as the hottest point on the chip (often called junction or hot spot temperature) and the memory. These can read higher than the core. Three things help in a small case: a shorter card that leaves room for air, a lower power draw, and a good cooler.
